Common questions

Will my photo be accepted?
School Foto checks head size, eye line, tilt, background, lighting and focus against the published rules for the size you picked, and names the authority those rules come from. It is a careful guide, not a guarantee — the office receiving your application decides. Check their own page before you go.
How many photos fit on one sheet?
A 4R holds 8 at 35 × 45 mm. Six 40 × 50 mm or six 2 × 2 in fit a 4R with Spacing set to Fit more, which fills the paper edge to edge — print that one borderless, or the outside photos lose about a millimetre.
My prints come out the wrong size.
Turn off "Scale to fit", "Fit to page" or "Shrink oversized pages" in the print dialog and print at 100%. Measure one photo with a ruler; if it is a few per cent small, scaling is still on somewhere.
The background removal took my glasses.
Use the Touch up brush on the Background step, set to Restore, and paint the frames back. Pinch to zoom in first — the canvas zooms so you can work on an edge properly.
I paid, and it is asking me to pay again.
Settings → Restore purchases. Make sure you are signed in with the same Apple Account you bought with. School Foto Pro is a single payment and works on every device signed into that account, and with Family Sharing.
Does School Foto upload my photos?
No. Everything runs on the phone; there is no server. See the privacy policy.
Why is my screenshot of the photo blank?
That is deliberate, the way banking apps hide their screens. Export or save the photo instead of screenshotting it.
