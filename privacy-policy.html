School Photo — Privacy Policy

Last updated 16 September 2026

School Photo collects nothing. There is no account, no analytics, no advertising, and no server. The app has no code that sends your photos anywhere.

Your photos

Photographs you take or choose are processed entirely on your device. Face detection, background removal and retouching all run on the phone itself, using Apple's Vision framework and a machine-learning model built into the app.

Photos you take in School Photo, and finished ID photos and print sheets you choose to save, are stored inside the app's own private storage on your device. Deleting the app deletes them. Nothing is copied off the device unless you choose to share, print or save it yourself, and then it goes only where you send it.

What is stored on your device

  • Photos you take in the app, so you can start again from a different shot.
  • Finished photos and print sheets you explicitly save.
  • Students you add, and what you enter about them — see below.
  • Your settings: preferred photo size, paper, theme, and similar choices.

Students you add

You can set up a student so their photos stay together and their print quantities are remembered. Everything you enter about a student stays on your device. It is written to the app's own private storage, alongside the photos, and it is never uploaded, never synced to any account, never shared with us, and never shared with anyone else. There is no server for it to go to.

A student record holds only what the app needs to do its job: the name you type, optionally a birth month and year, optionally a school and class, a colour, their photos, and how many copies of each size you want printed. The birth date is month and year only — a full date of birth is more of a child's data than this app needs, so it is not asked for.

All of it is optional except the name, and the name is only a label for you: call a student whatever you like. Deleting a student deletes their record and their photos. Deleting the app deletes all of it.

Permissions

  • Camera — only to take your photo. Asked for the first time you tap Take a photo, never at launch.
  • Photo library — choosing an existing photo uses Apple's system picker, which hands School Photo only the one photo you pick; the app never gets access to your library. Permission is requested only if you ask to save a photo back to Photos.

Purchases

School Photo Pro is bought through Apple's In-App Purchase. Apple processes the payment; School Photo never sees your payment details. The app asks Apple whether the purchase exists and stores nothing about it.

Children

School Photo is suitable for all ages and collects no personal information from anyone, including children. A parent using the app can enter a child's name and photograph — that information is stored only on the parent's own device, is never transmitted anywhere, and is never seen by us.

Changes
